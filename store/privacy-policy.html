Privacy Policy for Akshar Blocks

Last updated: 26 September 2026 (version 3)

Akshar Blocks is an English and Hindi alphabet and numbers learning game for young children, made by Akshar Blocks. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

We do not collect any information

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no in-app purchases. The app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send information anywhere.

What is saved on your device

To show progress, the app saves a few things only on your device:

  • the first name (or nickname) and picture you choose for each child in the parent area,
  • the stars each child has earned (stickers are unlocked from the star count),
  • the last day each child played and how many days in a row, for the daily streak,
  • which letters and numbers each child answered correctly or incorrectly in the games (the games use this to repeat what a child finds hard),
  • the voice speed setting.

This information is never sent anywhere, because the app has no internet access. You can change or delete it at any time in the parent area (reset progress or remove a child). Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The app is set up so that this information is not included in Android backups and is not copied when you move to a new phone.

Voice

The app reads letters and words aloud in English and Hindi using voice clips built into the app. For a few sentences that can't be prepared in advance, such as greeting your child by name, it uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (for example, Google's Speech Services); the app only sends the words it wants spoken to that engine on your phone. It does not record or listen to your voice, and it does not use the microphone.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We do not collect, use, or disclose any personal information from children. The parent area, where names are entered, is protected by a question for grown-ups.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any special permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage access).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
