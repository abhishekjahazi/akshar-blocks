Privacy Policy for Akshar Blocks

Last updated: 3 October 2026 (version 5)

Akshar Blocks is an English, Hindi and Marathi alphabet and numbers learning game for young children, made by Akshar Blocks. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

We do not collect any information

We (the makers of Akshar Blocks) do not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no in-app purchases. The only thing that uses the internet is the advertising described below.

Advertising

The app shows a small banner advertisement at the bottom of the home screen, provided by Google AdMob. There are no ads inside the games, and no full-screen or video ads.

Because the app is made for children, every ad request is marked as directed to children and as coming from a user under the age of consent, and ads are limited to the “G” (general audiences) rating. This means Google shows only ads that are not personalized: they are not based on what the child or family does in this or other apps. The app does not use the Android advertising ID or Android's ad-tracking features (it does not even ask for them).

To show an ad, Google's advertising service receives the information it needs to deliver it, such as the device's IP address, the type of device and its settings, and which ad was shown or tapped. Google handles this under its own policies: How Google uses information in ads and Google's requirements for apps directed to children. Nothing the child does in the games, and no names or progress, are ever sent with an ad request.

What is saved on your device

To show progress, the app saves a few things only on your device:

  • the first name (or nickname) and picture you choose for each child in the parent area,
  • the stars each child has earned (stickers are unlocked from the star count),
  • the last day each child played and how many days in a row, for the daily streak,
  • which letters and numbers each child answered correctly or incorrectly in the games (the games use this to repeat what a child finds hard),
  • how many minutes each child played on each of the last few weeks (for the report card), and today's total play time,
  • today's list of games for each child ("Today's games") and which of them are done,
  • which certificates each child has earned, and on what day,
  • the voice speed, game sounds, background music and daily play time settings.

This information is never sent anywhere: it is not part of ad requests and we have no servers to send it to. You can change or delete it at any time in the parent area (reset progress or remove a child). Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The app is set up so that this information is not included in Android backups and is not copied when you move to a new phone.

Sharing a report card or certificate

In the parent area you can choose to share a child's report card or a certificate as a picture (it shows the child's name). Children cannot share anything from the games. It is only sent if you tap Share and pick an app (for example WhatsApp or email); the report then goes wherever you send it. The app never sends anything by itself.

Voice

The app reads letters and words aloud in English, Hindi and Marathi using voice clips built into the app. For a few sentences that can't be prepared in advance, such as greeting your child by name, it uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (for example, Google's Speech Services); the app only sends the words it wants spoken to that engine on your phone. It does not record or listen to your voice, and it does not use the microphone.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We do not collect, use, or disclose any personal information from children, and ads are requested as described under Advertising. The parent area, where names are entered, is protected by a question for grown-ups.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to show the advertising banner. It does not ask for any special permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage access) and does not use the advertising ID.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
